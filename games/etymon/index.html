Etymon

Follow any word back six thousand years, and watch 190 languages trade words on one globe.

Every dot is a word and every line a step it took from an older word. Etymon reads Wiktionary and draws where English and Russian words came from: one word's family tree, a map of six thousand years, and a globe where each language is a flower and borrowed words arc between them. Count the spellings and the family tree of languages rebuilds itself. Count first letters and Grimm's law appears.
